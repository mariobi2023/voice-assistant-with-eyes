## Part 1 — Consent

**Project:** Voice Assistant With Eyes — a university project (Multimedia Database & Image
Processing, Fall 2026/2027) by Samer El Murr and Mario Bouissa.

**What you will do:** hold a phone that streams its camera to our laptop, ask a few questions
out loud ("What does the sign say?", "What is in front of me?") and listen to the answers.
It takes about 15 minutes. Then we ask you a few questions about your experience.

**Please know that:**
- This is a **student prototype**. It can be wrong. It is **not** a replacement for a white
  cane, a guide dog or a person. Never rely on it to decide if something is safe.
- A team member stays with you the whole time. The test is indoors, away from stairs and streets.
- The camera images are used only to answer your questions. **Faces are blurred** automatically,
  nobody is identified, and images are **deleted automatically after 30 minutes**.
- We keep only your questions, the answers and your feedback, **without your name**.
- You can **stop at any time**, without giving a reason.

**English:** I have understood the information above and I agree to take part.

**Français :** J'ai compris les informations ci-dessus et j'accepte de participer. Le prototype
peut se tromper ; il ne remplace ni une canne blanche, ni un chien guide. Les visages sont
floutés et les images sont effacées après 30 minutes. Je peux arrêter à tout moment.

**العربية:** لقد فهمت المعلومات أعلاه وأوافق على المشاركة. هذا النموذج الأولي قد يخطئ، ولا يحل
